Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Z.E Digital Tech ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the PRP-AI Assistant platform ("Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and organization name. We use this information to manage your account and communicate with you about the Service.

Code and Task Data: When you submit tasks to PRP-AI, we process the code, descriptions, and configurations you provide. This data is used solely to deliver the Service (agent processing, code generation, analysis, etc.).

Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the Service, including tasks submitted, agents used, API calls made, and dashboard interactions. This data helps us improve the Service and provide usage analytics on your dashboard.

Payment Information: Payment processing is handled through third-party providers (PayPal, bank transfer, or cryptocurrency networks). We do not store credit card numbers or full bank account details on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To process your tasks through our AI agents
  • To communicate with you about your account, billing, and service updates
  • To provide customer support
  • To generate anonymized, aggregated analytics to improve the platform
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

3. How We Do NOT Use Your Information

  • We do NOT use your code data to train AI models
  • We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties
  • We do NOT share your code with other customers
  • We do NOT use your data for advertising purposes

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Access to systems is controlled through role-based access and multi-factor authentication
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments are performed
  • Infrastructure is hosted in secure, certified data centers

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Task data and generated code are retained according to your plan:

  • Starter: 30 days task history
  • Professional: 1 year task history
  • Enterprise: Unlimited retention (configurable)

Upon account termination, your data is retained for 30 days to allow for export, after which it is permanently deleted. We may retain anonymized, aggregated data for analytics purposes.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use third-party services for infrastructure (Fly.io), payment processing, and email communications. These providers have access only to the data necessary to perform their functions and are obligated to protect it.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction. We will notify you before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Export: Request export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at elvizekaj@zedigital.tech.

8. On-Premise Deployment

Enterprise customers using on-premise deployment maintain full control over their data. In this configuration, no code data or task information is transmitted to our servers. Only license verification and optional anonymized usage metrics are communicated externally.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies for essential functionality (session management and preference storage). We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. We use basic analytics to understand how the website is used, which does not track individual users.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers, including contractual protections consistent with applicable data protection laws.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the Service at least 30 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised.
